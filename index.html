Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the public iPhone editions of My Journey Plan, My Journey Food, My Journey Sight, and My Journey Dream. Contact the developer at tinyworm@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

Trips, preparation lists, travel preferences, personal reviews, saved recommendations, and settings are stored locally by the relevant app. API keys are stored in the iPhone Keychain. We do not operate an app backend that receives these records or your keys. These apps do not include advertising or third-party behavioral analytics. Device backups and system diagnostics are governed by your Apple settings.

Optional AI services

You choose an AI provider and supply your own API key. Available integrations include OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Google Gemini, DeepSeek, Alibaba Qwen, Moonshot Kimi, Zhipu GLM, ByteDance Doubao (Volcano Engine), and Baidu ERNIE (Qianfan). Before AI requests containing your information can be sent, you must enable the data-sharing consent switch for that provider in Settings. You may withdraw consent there; this stops future requests but does not recall information already sent or cancel provider retention. Manual features and sample content remain available without AI consent. Features such as file processing and web research vary by provider.

AppInformation an AI request may contain
PlanSubmitted text, selected photos or documents, booking details, trip dates, destinations, addresses, and relevant itinerary context.
FoodLocation, travel mode, time, weather context, food requests, candidate place details, and personal reviews used for recommendations.
SightLocation, place context, text questions, and photos you submit for explanations.
DreamTravel profile, prior travel preferences, destinations, dates, companions, requirements, and journey context used for research.

Requests go directly from your device to the selected provider. The provider also receives your API credential for authentication, network information such as your IP address, and usage information. Providers may process or retain requests according to their API terms, your account settings, and applicable law, including outside your country. Web-assisted research may involve the provider's search services. Do not submit documents or photos that contain information you do not want shared.

Provider information: OpenAI · Anthropic · Gemini · DeepSeek · Qwen · Kimi · GLM · Doubao · ERNIE. Consult the terms and privacy information shown in your provider account before use. API charges are determined by your provider; the developer does not receive your API payments.

Apple services and permissions

Location is optional and supports nearby discovery, maps, weather, and trip-state checks. Apple Maps, geocoding, WeatherKit, speech recognition, calendar access, and navigation may communicate with Apple under Apple's policies. Microphone access is used when you start voice input; Apple's speech recognition may use remote processing depending on the device and language. Camera access is used when you choose to take a photo. Photo and file selection lets the app read the items you select; selected content is sent to AI only as part of an AI request after consent. Calendar access is used only for the app's calendar features. You can revoke system permissions in iPhone Settings.

Some journey research can continue at the AI provider while the app is not visible. A stop request is best effort; it does not reverse completed work or guarantee that no usage charges were incurred.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

You can delete editable records in the app, remove saved API keys where offered in Settings, and revoke a key directly with its provider. Deleting an app removes its local container; iOS Keychain entries or device backups may remain. For information already sent to a provider, use that provider's account controls or privacy request process. Contact us about privacy questions; do not email API keys, reservation documents, or other sensitive information. If you email support, we receive the message and address and use them to answer your request.

Children, accuracy, and changes

The apps are general travel tools and are not directed to children. AI output and demos can be inaccurate; important travel, health, safety, and entry information should be checked against official sources. We will update this page when practices change and revise the effective date.

您自行选择 AI 服务商，并提供自己的 API 密钥。可选接入包括 OpenAI、Anthropic Claude、Google Gemini、DeepSeek、阿里通义千问、月之暗面 Kimi、智谱 GLM、字节豆包（火山引擎）及百度文心（千帆）。发送包含您资料的 AI 请求前，必须在设置中开启针对所选服务商的数据共享同意开关。您可随时关闭以停止之后的请求；关闭不能撤回已发送内容，也不能取消服务商的留存。无需同意 AI 数据共享，也可以使用手动功能和演示内容。文件识别和联网研究等功能因服务商而异。

Apple 服务与系统权限

定位用于附近发现、地图、天气及旅程状态判断，可选择不授权。Apple 地图、地理编码、WeatherKit、语音识别、日历和导航功能可能依照 Apple 政策与其服务通信。麦克风在您开始语音输入时使用；Apple 语音识别是否在本机完成取决于设备及语言。相机在您选择拍照时使用。照片和文件选择器允许应用读取您选中的项目；选中内容仅在您同意后的 AI 请求中发送。日历权限仅用于日历相关功能。您可在 iPhone 设置中撤回权限。

部分旅程研究可能在应用不显示时继续由 AI 服务商处理。停止请求属于尽力执行，不能撤销已完成的处理，也不保证之前没有产生用量费用。

Troubleshooting / 常见问题

If an AI request fails, check the selected provider, key permissions, billing or quota, consent, and network. Search and document support varies by provider. Confirm extracted details before saving. To report a problem, include the app name, version, iPhone model, language, and steps to reproduce. Remove sensitive details from screenshots.

AI 请求失败时，请检查服务商、密钥权限、余额或额度、同意开关和网络。不同服务商的搜索及文件支持可能不同。提取结果请核对后保存。反馈问题请说明应用名称、版本、手机型号、语言及复现步骤，截图前遮盖敏感信息。
